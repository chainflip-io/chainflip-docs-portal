<Callout type="warning">
    **Check the decimals for the new assets.** `USDT` on BSC has **18 decimals**,
    unlike `USDT` on every other supported chain, which has 6. `cbBTC` has **8
    decimals**, like BTC, rather than the 18 used by most Ethereum ERC-20s. Use
    [Get Assets](/brokers/javascript-sdk/functions#get-assets.md "mention") to
    read the decimals of an asset programmatically rather than assuming them per
    ticker.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
    `BNB`, `USDT` on BSC and `cbBTC` ship without an oracle price feed. This
    means they cannot be used as lending collateral, are not eligible for
    oracle-based [trading strategies](/lp/passive-liquidity), and have no default
    oracle price slippage protection applied to swaps.
</Callout>

## Chainflip Networks

We currently support different networks for different purposes:

- **Mainnet**: Our mainnet deployment. Learn more in [Mainnet Documentation](/validators/mainnet/validator-setup#1-prerequisites).
- **Perseverance**: Our persistent testnet. Learn more in [Perseverance Documentation](/validators/testnet/validator-setup).
- **Sisyphos**: Our staging network where we test releases before upgrading Perseverance.
